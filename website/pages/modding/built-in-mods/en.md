# Built-in mods and their settings

These mods live in the repository's `mods/` folder. Open a mod page to see its purpose, target processes, and setting groups. Every setting has its own page with type, default, and allowed values. Mod settings live in `extended.mod.json` and are separate from general Modloader preferences.

| Mod | What it does | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SF Auto Stamina Refill](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-auto-stamina-refill) | Repeatedly refills stamina to maximum. | 1 setting page |
| [SF Infinite Item Split](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-infinite-item-split) | Keeps the original stack when splitting items. Also affects other inventory operations. | no player settings |
| [SF Infinite Item Use](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-infinite-item-use) | Prevents items from being consumed when used. | no player settings |
| [SF No Fall Damage](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-no-fall-damage) | Prevents health loss from falling. | no player settings |
| [SF No Resource Cost](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-no-resource-cost) | Removes resource consumption in supported building, crafting, and item-use actions. | no player settings |
| [SF Production Time](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-production-time) | Sets timed production recipes to a chosen base duration. World speed settings still apply. | 1 setting page |
| [SF Unlimited Flight](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-unlimited-flight) | Lets you keep flying without a time limit. | no player settings |
| [SF Unlock Blueprints](#doc-builtin-mod-sf-unlock-blueprints) | Changes recipe unlock requirements to the first Flame Altar hint. | no player settings |
| [World Editor](#doc-builtin-mod-world-editor) | Copy, rotate, save, and paste parts of the world. | 37 setting pages |
